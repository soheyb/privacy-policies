 Privacy Policy — VoltPro: Cable Size Calculator

  Last updated: August 7, 2026

  1. Overview

  VoltPro ("the App") is developed and operated by Soheyb Alouach ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

  We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. The App runs entirely on your device, 100% offline, with no ads, no account, and no trackers.

  2. Information We Process

  2.1 Information stored locally on your device

  The App stores the following data exclusively on your device (local storage):

  - Your settings: display language, conductor material, preferences
  - The electrical panels you compose and your saved worksites (worksite name, circuits)
  - The PRO key unlock status
  - A local usage counter (triggering, once, the native Google Play review prompt)

  This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

  2.2 Device language

  On first launch, the App reads your device's configured language, locally, solely to display the interface in your language (French, English, Portuguese, or Romanian). This information is not transmitted to anyone.

  2.3 Exported PDF documents

  Panel reports (PDF) are generated locally on your device. Printing or sharing them happens exclusively at your initiative, through your device's own features.

  3. Third-Party Services

  3.1 Google Play Billing (In-App Purchase)

  The App offers a single one-time in-app purchase ("PRO key") that unlocks certain features for life. This transaction is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store any payment information. Billing data is governed by Google Play's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

  This is the only third-party service used by the App. No advertising, analytics, or tracking service is integrated.

  4. Data We Do Not Collect

  We do not collect:

  - Your name, email address, or any contact information
  - Account credentials (the App has no accounts)
  - Location data
  - Photos, contacts, or other device files
  - Advertising identifiers
  - Usage statistics, analytics, or crash data through our own servers — we operate no servers
