# Privacy Policy — IronLog: Workout Tracker

**Last updated:** August 13, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

IronLog: Workout Tracker ("the App") is developed and operated by **Soheyb Alouach** ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the App and your rights regarding that information.

The App is built **local-first**: it contains no advertising SDK, no account system, and no server of ours. **Your training data lives on your device, and nowhere else.** The only data we receive are feature-level usage statistics (see section 3.3), which you can disable — never the content of your workouts.

---

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data **exclusively on your device**, in a local SQLite database:

- Workout history, exercises, sets, reps, and perceived effort (RPE)
- Training programs and planned workouts
- Coaching suggestions and progression state (computed on-device by a deterministic algorithm — no data is sent to any AI service)
- Personal records and performance statistics
- Body measurements (weight, body fat, and other optional metrics)
- Training streaks and session logs
- App settings and preferences (language, weight unit, theme, notifications)

This data never leaves your device unless **you** explicitly export it (see Section 4).

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

### 3.1 App store billing (In-App Purchases)

The App offers optional premium purchases. Payments are processed entirely by **Google Play** (Android) or the **Apple App Store** (iOS). We do not receive or store your payment information. Billing data is governed by [Google Play's Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/about/play-terms/) and [Apple's Media Services Terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/).

### 3.2 wger Workout Manager (Exercise Library)

The App optionally fetches exercise data from the open-source **wger Workout Manager** API ([wger.de](https://wger.de)) to enrich the exercise library with descriptions and images. Only anonymous catalog requests are made — no personal data is sent to this service.

### 3.3 Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

Since version 4.3, the App uses **Google Firebase Analytics** to understand which features are used. This collects **usage milestones only** (for example: "a workout was saved", "the coach tab was opened") together with device-level identifiers assigned by Firebase, device model, and country. It **never** collects the content of your training: no exercises, no weights, no reps, no body measurements, no personal records — none of your training data ever leaves your device.

You can turn analytics off at any time in **Settings → Usage statistics**. When disabled, nothing is sent. Firebase processing is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 4. Data Export and Backup

You can export your data at any time (JSON backup or CSV) from the App's settings. Exports are generated on your device and shared only through the share destination **you** choose (e.g. your own cloud drive, email, or files app). We never receive a copy.

---

## 5. Data We Do Not Collect

We do **not** collect:

- Your name, email address, or any contact information
- Account credentials or login data (there is no account)
- Location data
- Advertising identifiers (the App contains no ads and no ad SDK)
- The content of your workouts, through analytics or otherwise (usage statistics are feature-level milestones only, and can be disabled — see section 3.3)
- Photos, contacts, or other device files (progress photos, if you add them, stay on your device)
- Any data through our own servers — we operate none

---

## 6. Notifications

The App may send local notifications to remind you of scheduled workouts. These are scheduled entirely on your device and involve no external server. You can disable them at any time in the App or your device settings.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly target children under the age of 13. Usage statistics (section 3.3) are the only data collected, and they contain no personal content.

---

## 8. Data Retention

All training data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time from the App's settings or by uninstalling the App. Usage statistics (section 3.3) are retained by Firebase Analytics for up to 14 months.

---

## 9. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Your training data lives on your device — you hold it directly. For usage statistics (section 3.3), you can stop collection at any time in Settings, and you may request deletion of analytics data by contacting us at the address below. Requests related to billing data should be directed to Google or Apple.
