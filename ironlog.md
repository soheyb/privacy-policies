## 1. Overview

IronLog: Workout Tracker ("the App") is developed and operated by **Soheyb Alouach** ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. **We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information on our own servers.** The App runs entirely on your device.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information stored locally on your device

The App stores the following data **exclusively on your device** using a local SQLite database:

- Workout history, exercises, sets, and reps
- Personal records and performance statistics
- Body measurements (weight, body fat, and other optional metrics)
- Training streak and session logs
- App settings and preferences (language, weight unit, notifications)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

### 2.2 Information collected by third-party services

The App integrates third-party services that may collect data independently. We do not control this data collection.

## 3. Third-Party Services

### 3.1 Google AdMob

The free version of the App displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob** (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and use the following information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads:

- Advertising ID (GAID on Android)
- Device information (model, OS version, language)
- IP address (approximate location)
- App usage data and interactions with ads

We have configured AdMob to request user consent where required (GDPR). Users in applicable regions will be shown a consent form before ads are served.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses data from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Opt-out:** You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your device settings:  
Android: *Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization*

### 3.2 Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase ("IronLog Premium") to unlock additional features and remove advertisements. This purchase is processed entirely by **Google Play**. We do not receive or store your payment information. All billing data is governed by [Google Play's Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/about/play-terms/).

### 3.3 wger Workout Manager (Exercise Library)

The App optionally syncs exercise data from the open-source **wger Workout Manager** API ([wger.de](https://wger.de)) to enrich the exercise library with descriptions and images. No personal data is sent to this service — only anonymous requests for exercise catalog data.

## 4. Data We Do Not Collect

We do **not** collect:

- Your name, email address, or any contact information
- Account credentials or login data
- Precise GPS location
- Photos, contacts, or other device files
- Any data through our own servers or databases

## 5. Notifications

The App may send local push notifications to remind you of scheduled workouts or to alert you when your training streak is at risk. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any external server. You can disable them at any time in your device settings.

Since all workout and personal data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App. We retain no data on our end.
