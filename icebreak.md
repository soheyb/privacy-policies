# Privacy Policy for Icebreak

  **Last updated:** April 10, 2026

  ## Introduction

  Icebreak is a mobile application designed to help you write the perfect first message on dating apps. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains clearly what data is used,
  how, and why.

  ## Data Storage

  Icebreak stores data **locally on your device only** (via AsyncStorage). This includes:

  - Your saved favorite messages
  - Your language preference
  - Your premium status

  This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server. It is deleted if you uninstall the app.

  ## Third-Party Services

  ### In-App Purchases

  Purchases are handled exclusively by **Apple (App Store)** or **Google (Google Play)**. We have no access to any payment information. Apple's and Google's privacy policies apply to
  transactions.

  ## Data We Do Not Collect

  - We do not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information
  - We do not use analytics or crash reporting tools (no Firebase, Sentry, etc.)
  - We do not track your location
  - We do not share any data with third parties beyond what is described above
  - We do not require an account or login

  ## Advertising

  The app does not display any advertisements.

  ## Children's Privacy

  Icebreak is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

  ## Changes to This Policy

  We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
