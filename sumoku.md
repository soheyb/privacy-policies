# Privacy Policy — Sumoku: Killer Sudoku

**Last updated:** April 28, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Sumoku: Killer Sudoku ("the App") is developed and operated by **Soheyb Alouach** ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. **We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information on our own servers.** The App runs entirely on your device.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information stored locally on your device

The App stores the following data **exclusively on your device** using local storage:

- Game progress and puzzle history
- Personal best times and statistics
- App settings and preferences (theme, language, sound)
- Daily streak and challenge completion status

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

### 2.2 Information collected by third-party services

The App integrates third-party services that may collect data independently. We do not control this data collection.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

### 3.1 Google AdMob

The App displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob** (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and use the following information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads:

- Advertising ID (GAID on Android)
- Device information (model, OS version, language)
- IP address (approximate location)
- App usage data and interactions with ads

We have configured AdMob to request **non-personalized ads only**, which limits the use of your data for ad targeting.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses data from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Opt-out:** You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your device settings:  
Android: *Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization*

### 3.2 Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove advertisements ("Remove Ads"). This purchase is processed entirely by **Google Play**. We do not receive or store your payment information. All billing data is governed by [Google Play's Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/about/play-terms/).

---

## 4. Data We Do Not Collect

We do **not** collect:

- Your name, email address, or any contact information
- Account credentials or login data
- Precise GPS location
- Photos, contacts, or other device files
- Any data through our own servers or databases

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly target children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps. The App's ad serving complies with Google's [Families Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335).

---

## 6. Data Retention

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App. We retain no data on our end.

---

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- Access, correct, or delete personal data related to you
- Object to or restrict certain data processing
- Withdraw consent at any time

Since we do not collect personal data directly, any requests related to data processed by Google AdMob or Google Play should be directed to Google. For any other inquiries, contact us at the address below.

## 8. Security
